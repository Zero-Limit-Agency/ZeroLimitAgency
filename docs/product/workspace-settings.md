# Workspace settings — product rules

The Core settings page is shared by Personal, Creator and Team.

- Personal shows space name, immutable profession, owner and lifecycle. Its owner can rename and archive/restore.
- Creator shows its Core identity, owner and lifecycle. Its owner can rename and archive/restore. There is no public bio, portfolio or ordering UI.
- Team shows organization name, slug, canonical owner, viewer role and Team-space lifecycle. The primary Team display name always mirrors organization name. Only the canonical owner can change structural settings; admins retain operational member/invitation duties only.

A Team slug change is immediate, unique and server validated. It may affect future public URLs or integrations; this version creates no redirect history.

The interface is bilingual FR/EN with French fallback, explicit labels, live status feedback, confirmation before archive, visible read-only explanations and controls at least 44px high. Layouts collapse at tablet and phone widths down to 320px. The visual treatment stays black/plum/burgundy, ivory and muted pink, with no dashboard redesign.
